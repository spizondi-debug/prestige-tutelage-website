# Prestige Tutelage — 12-day social media campaign plan

**Campaign:** Prestige Tutelage — Skills That Work
**Owner:** Prestige Tutelage (Pty) Ltd
**Posts:** 12 · one per campaign day
**Primary call to action:** prestigetutelage.co.za
**Language:** South African English (en-ZA)

## Channels

| Channel | Handling |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Posted manually from the LinkedIn manual-posting guide |
| Facebook | Prepared later through Metricool |
| TikTok | Prepared later through Metricool |
| YouTube | Prepared later through Metricool (Shorts) |

Nothing in this campaign is scheduled or published by the build. Every asset is
produced as a file for a person to post.

## Asset specification

| Asset | Specification |
|---|---|
| Square poster | 1080 x 1080 PNG |
| Vertical poster | 1080 x 1920 PNG |
| Video | 1080 x 1920 MP4, H.264, 30fps, 15-25s, silent, on-screen text only |

## Brand

- **Logo** — `public/prestige-tutelage-logo.png`. Original supplied artwork. Never redrawn, recoloured or substituted.
- **Blue** `#006FD8` · **Green** `#2DA22F` · **Navy** `#0B2742`
- Blue and green are sampled directly from the original logo artwork so every poster sits in exact harmony with the mark. blueDeep, ink, body, cloud and line are the repository's own tokens from tailwind.config.js.
- **Typography** — Poppins, weights 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800. Poppins matches the geometric sans of the original logo wordmark.
- **Tagline** — Passion for training

## Compliance rules applied to every post

- No invented statistics, completion rates, learner numbers or client counts.
- No named clients, testimonials or logos that are not supplied and approved.
- No AI-generated people. Only the five genuine photographs already in the repository.
- SAQA IDs, NQF levels and credits are quoted only where the repository holds a verified value.
- Short courses are never described as accredited or credit-bearing.
- B-BBEE posts claim Level 1 contributor status only, with no guarantee of client scorecard outcomes.
- Prestige is never presented as a B-BBEE verification agency.

Standard availability wording used across programme posts:

> Programme availability is subject to current qualification registration, Prestige Tutelage's applicable accreditation or approved delivery route, learner eligibility, workplace requirements and the relevant assessment and certification arrangements.
