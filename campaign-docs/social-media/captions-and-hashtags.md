```
What a learnership actually is, in plain terms.

It is a structured, work-based route to a registered qualification. Classroom learning is combined with real workplace experience, under a formal agreement between the learner, the employer and the training provider.

An 18.2 learnership creates that structure for unemployed learners — proper learning, proper workplace exposure, and human support that keeps people engaged and progressing rather than dropping out halfway.

For employers, Prestige carries the load: learner sourcing, screening, onboarding, learning agreements, induction, delivery, workplace coordination, attendance, assessments, monitoring and reporting.

Learner eligibility, programme availability and enrolment conditions apply.
```
